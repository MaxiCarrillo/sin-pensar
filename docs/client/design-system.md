# Client design system

## Current evidence

- `src/index.css` owns the white light-mode base, semantic color/spacing/radius/shadow tokens, reset, and global focus/reduced-motion rules. `--radius-control-pill: 999px` is the shared capsule radius for ordinary controls, `--space-shadow-gutter` reserves reusable space around shadow-bearing controls inside clipped regions, and the primary-tinted `--shadow-edge-control` keeps its 12px horizontal blur within the minimum route gutter. Ant Design large controls retain the compact default-equivalent `40px` height.
- Colocated CSS Modules own product layout and visual composition. Ant Design owns behavior-rich `Button`, `Input`, `Drawer`, and `Tooltip` controls; `src/core/theme/theme.ts` maps the Sin Pensar palette into `ThemeConfig` and scopes `borderRadius`, `borderRadiusSM`, and `borderRadiusLG` capsule overrides to `Button` and `Input` only. Explicit circle icon buttons remain circles, while cards, avatars, option tiles, list rows, drawers, panels, summaries, tables, and other containers retain their shape-specific radii.
- `html` and `body` own the full-width viewport background. `--mobile-breakpoint` is the single interactive application-shell width token and is currently `390px`; `#root` is centered and capped to that width on every route.
- Shared bottom drawers keep their Ant Design mask and panel at viewport level while a centered child content container is capped to the mobile shell width.
- The implemented palette is white with primary `#00AAB7`, secondary `#DE2C6E`, and tertiary `#11B087`. No dark algorithm is configured.
- Spanish product copy, labeled icon controls, native avatar radios, status announcements, and non-color roster feedback are present. Automated browser accessibility and viewport tests are not configured.
- Team assignment uses vertically stacked table-like cards with cycling semantic headers, one-column player rows, explicit remove-to-unassigned controls, and a shared bottom drawer for keyboard-accessible player selection. Team-count choices live in the Players configuration drawer and describe the balanced sizes produced by the finalized roster.
- The Players configuration drawer renders turn duration, main-deck quota, and roster-derived team counts as scoped Ant Design radio cards. Deck and team choices are typography-first, with a prominent count and compact text instead of decorative icons; team summaries still come from the shared balancing helper. Unselected cards keep a white surface with a 3px primary border; selected cards use a primary surface, white content, an upper-right check icon, and accessible selected text. Legends have a small scoped gap before their option grids, and pointer hover does not change card position, fill, border, or text color. Time and quota groups use three equal columns, while team options use a wrapping two-column grid. These option cards deliberately retain a 16px card radius instead of the ordinary-control pill token.
- Private card selection uses a full-height `/cards` shell. Each player sees a private handoff, a dedicated main screen with all 10 dealt cards, and then a dedicated lightning screen whose rendered list is derived by filtering out every main-card ID before mapping. Handoffs mount no card records; saving removes the private card list before the next handoff. Revealed cards scroll inside the bounded content region while phase-specific progress and actions remain visible. Every card is a semantic button with text state, `aria-pressed`, visible focus, selected opacity `1`, and unselected opacity `0.4`. Both Principal and Relámpago selections use the same semantic green glow/shadow while their visible badges preserve non-color role feedback.
- Draft and gameplay cards share a borderless `16px` white shell, exact card shadow, blank top cap, and white gutters around three full-width semantic bands: title `#DE2C6E`, character `#11B087`, and everything `#00AAB7`. At normal card heights, every colored band is a positioning context: its wrapped value is geometrically centered against the full band, while its wrapped subtype is removed from layout flow and anchored at the lower right with bounded width and right-aligned text. The centered `PALABRA CLAVE` label uses the exact muted card token `hsla(225, 6%, 59%, 1)`; the centered keyword value uses primary `#00AAB7` while preserving the established size and weight hierarchy. Draft selection remains borderless and adds only the semantic selected shadow—never a selected border or pseudo-element—so selection causes no layout shift.
- Gameplay uses a full-height `/game` shell with private handoff, active turn, timeout, stage-complete, game-complete, and results views. Handoff preserves player/privacy messaging, then presents only the stage name as its round heading above one compact, divided information strip with equal duration, attempts, and rule columns. Its primary-colored visuals share a `44px` optical box and use standard Ant Design icons: `FieldTimeOutlined` with the single visible and accessible seconds value absolutely centered over its clock face on a small surface-colored pill, an overlapping `UserOutlined` plus `MessageOutlined` speaking-person composition, and the unchanged `LikeOutlined`. Small muted category labels support stronger fact values. Complete `Podés` / `No podés` guidance uses vertically centered labels, compact description rows, and subtle dividers before the team/card/score or Relámpago control context, without mounting a catalog record. The shared stage definition still owns the concise rule shown as the third fact and in active/next-stage contexts; the information region scrolls independently of the bottom reveal action, and reveal explicitly starts the deadline.
- The active gameplay shell is a three-row grid: round information at the top, a `minmax(0, 1fr)` card region in the middle, and the visible `Pasar`/`Acertó` actions in an ordinary-flow bottom row. The middle region and nested card stack use explicit `min-height: 0`; the card fills only that available region up to its established maximum, so short heights cannot push the actions over the card. A block-size container query compacts only gameplay cards when the card stack's content box is at most `469px`: it reduces gaps, padding, and type with bounded container-relative values and moves subtypes into a dedicated flow row so wrapped values cannot overlap them. With the stack's `12px` padding, that content-box threshold corresponds to a `493px` outer stack. The compact geometry requires `214px`; `216px` is the practical supported content-box minimum (`240px` outer stack, roughly a `434px` viewport with the current shell chrome, before larger safe-area insets or wrapped round information). At a `215px` content box (`239px` outer stack) and below, the active card becomes a bounded vertical scrollport with `touch-action: pan-y`; native touch panning intentionally replaces touch-swipe decisions there, so the visible 48px action buttons provide touch decisions while pointer cancellation clears transient drag state. Keyboard decisions and focus styling remain available, and each new `card.id` resets the reused active article to `scrollTop = 0` before focus without remounting outgoing snapshots. Above that fallback, Pointer Events continue mapping dominant downward movement to correct and upward movement to pass; ArrowDown/ArrowUp and visible `Acertó`/`Pasar` buttons provide equivalent controls. The active gameplay card and its outgoing overlays otherwise reuse the same card-face contents and preserve the shared borderless shell and keyword hierarchy without draft selection opacity, badge, or press semantics. A decision synchronously updates gameplay. Pointer-origin snapshots retain the release transform and pair a 180ms ease-out visual with a 180ms interaction lock; button and keyboard snapshots pair a 1800ms control-origin visual with only a 320ms interaction lock. Monotonic local IDs and per-overlay completion let multiple control visuals coexist, while reduced motion creates no outgoing overlay or timer and completes interaction synchronously. Text status distinguishes both outcomes. Stage or game completion may unmount this route-owned visual before it finishes; no cross-route animation portal is used.
- Active and outgoing gameplay cards share one portrait `aspect-ratio: 9 / 16` declaration. The stack owns `var(--space-sm)` (`12px`) internal padding under the global border-box rule, leaving its named size container's content box as the sizing box for both cards. Their responsive border box follows `W = min(I, 300px, 9B / 16)` and `H = 16W / 9`, where `I` and `B` are that inner content box's inline and block sizes. Container query dimensions and `cqh` units also use this content box. The padding contains the unchanged `2px 4px 8px` card-face shadow, and automatic margins keep active and outgoing cards at the same centered size; the middle region continues clipping outgoing motion before the separate action row.
- Active timer progress is a fixed viewport solid stage-color overlay scaled vertically from the bottom by `--timer-progress-scale`, so the color rises without layout reflow. Visible seconds and bounded live announcements are derived from the absolute deadline. Timeout uses secondary `#DE2C6E`; game completion uses primary `#00AAB7`.
- Card completion, gameplay completion/results, and Relámpago attribution keep vertical scrolling and explicit horizontal containment while expanding their scrollports into the available route or Drawer gutter. Internal shadow gutters keep control and table shadows inside those clipping boundaries, including extra block-end room for the downward control shadow. The Relámpago selection layout preserves the existing card-scroller and footer width while its header reclaims the route gutters before reserving symmetric space around balanced 40px columns and a centered title; its back control uses `--shadow-edge-control` without replacing the visible focus outline. Interactive Drawer player rows remain capped to the 390px content shell, and the Cancel action stays outside the list scrollport.
- Gameplay pages use `100vh` with a `100dvh` enhancement, safe-area padding, bounded internal scrolling, wrapping names, and bottom actions within the shared 390px shell. Automated domain/source checks exist, but browser accessibility and 320px/390px visual inspection have not been performed.

## Target rules

- Use a white `#FFF` background with primary `#00AAB7`, secondary `#DE2C6E`, and tertiary `#11B087`. Express reusable roles through semantic custom properties in `src/index.css`, not scattered literals.
- Use `*.module.css` colocated with components. Keep global CSS limited to tokens, reset/base behavior, and intentional application-wide rules.
- Keep viewport backgrounds full-width on `html`/`body` or a viewport-level background layer. Center and cap interactive route content at `width: min(var(--mobile-breakpoint), 100%)` through `#root`; anchor shell actions to that boundary and do not add route-specific desktop max-widths.
- Use El Topo's compact layout scale as the Home and drawer baseline: `1rem` shell and medium spacing, `1.5rem` large spacing, `2rem` extra-bold page titles, `0.75rem` Home supporting text, and `1.25rem` bold drawer titles. Preserve Sin Pensar's identity through the compact bordered Home card, attached gradient question-mark emblem, short title divider, palette, content, and frontend-only flow.
- Keep the Home card visually centered against the viewport by overlaying the bottom-anchored footer on the page grid, reserving equal block clearance around the centering area, and including the attached emblem's overhang in the centered box. Let short viewports grow and scroll rather than clipping overflow.
- Prefer Ant Design for established control behavior and apply product-specific CSS Module classes through component and semantic `classNames`; keep reusable theme decisions centralized.
- Render ordinary buttons and text inputs as capsules through `--radius-control-pill` and the scoped Ant Design `Button`/`Input` size tokens. Keep icon-only controls explicit circles, and never apply the control token to card faces or shape-specific interactive tiles and containers.
- Use Ant Design bottom drawers with mask and Escape closing, focus trapping/restoration, `closable={false}`, no visual close control or default header, and an accessible centered `hgroup` title plus description. Keep the mask and panel viewport-level, and cap the drawer's child content container to `--mobile-breakpoint`.
- Compose avatar selection and nickname entry through the shared `AvatarInput`: the avatar opens a keyboard-operable bottom drawer and the controlled Ant Design input keeps clear, count, prefix, required, status, and help semantics stable.
- Preserve Spanish product copy. Technical names, comments, and docs remain English.
- Use semantic HTML, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, accessible names, practical touch targets, and feedback that does not depend on color alone.
- Design explicit idle, transition, empty, active, paused, completed, recoverable-error, and destructive-confirmation states when applicable.
- Check at least 320px and 390px widths, plus wider layouts, for overflow, long names, zoom, and touch usability. Do not claim responsiveness or accessibility without evidence.
